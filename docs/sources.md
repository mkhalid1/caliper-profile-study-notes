## Unsure or inconsistent

- **Trait count.** Caliper's profile page says 22 traits; its FAQ says 21 behavioural traits plus abstract reasoning; Talogy's glossary lists 22 including Abstract Reasoning. These agree once Abstract Reasoning is counted as a trait. Caliper's published sample report shows 21 trait bars and leaves out Stress Tolerance, so the exact list on a given report may vary.
- **Languages.** The FAQ list has 27 entries, the profile page counter shows 24, and the sales page says 32. We quote the FAQ list and suggest asking the employer.
- **Likert scale labels.** Talogy says the scale runs from one to seven but does not publish the labels. The labels in our practice items are our convention.

## What is our own guidance

These parts are our advice, not Caliper's or Talogy's: all practice items and worked examples, the methods and traps lists, the "typical week at work" frame, the grouping of traits in the traits guide, the study plans, the diagnostic thresholds and the suggestion to block 75 minutes.
