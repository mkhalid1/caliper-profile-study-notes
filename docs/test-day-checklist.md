# Test-day checklist

A short list for the day you take the Caliper Profile. It is untimed, but it is long, so most of this is about protecting a calm, uninterrupted hour or more.

## When the link arrives

- [ ] Check the email for an expiry date. Caliper lets employers set the link to stop working after a set number of days.
- [ ] Check whether it is the full profile or a shortened version. The full one takes about an hour; Talogy says most people finish in 60 to 75 minutes. The short one takes about 25 minutes and has no reasoning section.
- [ ] If English is not your strongest language, ask the employer whether another language is available.
- [ ] If the link does not work, contact the employer that sent it. Caliper's FAQ says the referring company is the one to help.

## The day before

- [ ] Do a few reasoning items as a warm-up. Not a full study session.
- [ ] Write your frame in one sentence: "me at work, on a typical week."
- [ ] Sleep. An hour of honest self-description is more tiring than it sounds.

## Before you start

- [ ] Block **75 minutes** in a quiet place, with no meetings or calls after it.
- [ ] Use a device and connection you trust. Caliper says the profile is online and mobile friendly, but a laptop with a steady connection is the calmest option for an hour.
- [ ] Have scrap paper and a pen for the reasoning items, unless the instructions say otherwise.
- [ ] Turn off notifications. Have water nearby.

## During the test

- [ ] Read every instruction screen. The real scale labels are on screen; follow them, not your memory of practice items.
- [ ] Forced choice: pick "most like me" first, then the clearest "least like me". Move on quickly.
- [ ] Likert: read for negatives ("rarely", "not"), choose agree or disagree, then how strongly.
- [ ] Keep one frame all the way through. If you notice yourself answering as you are at home, or as the job might want, reset to your sentence.
- [ ] Reasoning items: name the rule before you look at the options. Guess rather than leave one blank.
- [ ] If you need a break, finish the section you are in first. Caliper says progress is saved at the last completed section.

## If something goes wrong

- [ ] If your connection drops, reopen the link. Caliper says the assessment resumes after the last section you completed.
- [ ] If it still will not load, contact the employer, not Caliper.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Do not expect a results email. Caliper does not send results to test takers; the employer decides whether to share them.
- [ ] If you want feedback, ask the employer politely. Knowing the [traits](traits.md) will help you follow the conversation.

## Last practice before the real one

A day or two before, try a longer run with feedback on every answer: the [free Caliper Profile practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/caliper-profile?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=56kub3&utm_content=caliper-profile).
