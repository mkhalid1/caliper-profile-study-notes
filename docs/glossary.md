# Glossary

Terms you will meet in Caliper's materials, in your invitation or in these notes. The 22 traits themselves are defined in the [traits guide](traits.md).

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Abstract reasoning** | The one cognitive ability the Caliper Profile measures, using number patterns and shape analogies. The only items with right answers. |
| **Behaviour** | A visible action Caliper predicts from a combination of traits, such as "demonstrates an openness to learning new things". |
| **Caliper Essentials** | Caliper's suite of reports for selection, coaching and development. |
| **Caliper Predictor Plus** | Talogy's name for the shortened, roughly 25-minute version, which has no abstract reasoning section. |
| **Competency** | An outcome area for a role, built from several behaviours, such as Learning Agility. |
| **Debrief consultation** | A conversation with a Caliper expert about someone's results, offered to employers as an add-on. |
| **Experimental items** | New questions included in the profile so they can be tested before becoming part of it. |
| **Forced choice** | A format where you pick the statement most like you and the one least like you from a set. Most of the Caliper Profile uses it. |
| **Highlights report** | A short Caliper report with competency scores only, for recruiters. |
| **Job-fit score** | A score from 1 to 99 predicting how likely someone is to succeed in a job model. |
| **Job model** | A set of 5 to 9 competencies that matter for a type of job. Caliper lists 52 validated models, 11 for sales. |
| **Likert item** | A statement you rate on a 1 to 7 agreement scale. |
| **Norm group** | A representative sample of test takers. Caliper compares your answers with a norm group for your country of employment. |
| **Percentile** | Where your score sits relative to the norm group. A trait score of 50 is average. |
| **Talogy** | The company that now publishes the Caliper assessment, as Talogy Caliper. |
| **Trait** | A personality motivation or natural tendency. Caliper scores 21 of them, plus Abstract Reasoning. |
